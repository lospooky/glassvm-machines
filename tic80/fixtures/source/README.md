# Fixture sources

`public/game.tic` is the unmodified upstream Gecko cartridge, retaining its
upstream filename, at revision `f958fd19218510468a36f93b77449ca26a2d13cf`.
It is byte-identical to `../paper/game.tic` and `../smoke.rom`.
`upstream-luademo.lua` is the official TIC-80 Lua demo at revision
`4aba09c98f1e5028b82765be1647677b08d35942`.

The 13 distributable entries in the E3 paper cohort have their source material
under `source/e3/`; the other 17 E3 members are metadata-only and have no cart
bytes in this repository.
