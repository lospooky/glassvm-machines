# Fixture licences

The generated smoke ROM contains no third-party game code. Its provenance uses
`LicenseRef-Program-Diffusion-Generated` to identify repository-authored test
material without asserting an unrelated upstream licence.

The paper-only ROMs have separate upstream terms and are not covered by the
repository's BSD-3-Clause license. `paper/cohort.toml` records CE1's
per-ROM licensing: IBM Logo Test is GPL-3.0; Horse World Online and Octojam 2
Title are CC0-1.0. The E3 artifacts are the CC0-1.0 programs from the pinned
John Earnest chip8Archive cohort; its revision, license evidence, source URLs,
and hashes are recorded in `../paper/e3/cohort.json`.
