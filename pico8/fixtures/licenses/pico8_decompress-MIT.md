# Fixture provenance

`reference.p8` and `reference.p8.png` are copied unchanged from the
MIT-licensed `pico8_decompress` 0.1.0 crate by Shane Celis. The crate describes
its decompressor as a port of C snippets published by Dan Sanderson and Joseph
White.

`reference.p8.rom` is the first 32 KiB of the cartridge payload mechanically
extracted from `reference.p8.png`; it contains the same fixture cartridge in
the documented raw-ROM representation.

Upstream: <https://github.com/shanecelis/pico8_decompress>
