PICO-8 paper fixtures have per-artifact licenses; they are not covered by the
repository's BSD-3-Clause code license.

- The smoke and Fire Intro source fixtures are MIT licensed. Their copied
  license text and provenance are retained alongside them.
- The 29 Fraser-derived PICO-8 E3 carts are marked individually as CC BY-NC-SA
  4.0 by the source dataset. Their attribution, source, license, and digest are
  in `../paper/e3/ATTRIBUTION.md` and `../paper/e3/cohort.json`. Distribution
  and reuse must follow the attribution, noncommercial, and ShareAlike terms.
- The adapted Fire Intro E3 cart carries the upstream MIT license; the title
  text normalization is documented in its provenance and attribution.
